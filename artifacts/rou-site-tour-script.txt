ROU SITE TOUR — read-aloud
Target: 35–45 seconds. Features do not have to be live.
Voice: Carina / whoever is on camera. Speak as Rou.

Pace: about 150 words a minute. Do not rush. Pause on the em dashes.


PRIMARY — about 40 seconds (~100 words)

Hi — I’m Rou. I’ll help you find your place in Aiken.

Start on the map. Move it. Filter if you want. When a home feels right — tap it.

Ask me what’s nearby — playgrounds, public schools, a grocery run — or what’s true about this house. I only say what we know.

Like a home? Save it. Want to walk through? Request a showing.

I’m here on the page the whole time. Nick is one tap away.


LONG — about 45 seconds (~112 words)

Hi — I’m Rou. I’ll help you find your place in Aiken.

Start on the map. Move it until the streets feel right. Filter if you want. When a home catches you — tap it. Open it.

Ask me what’s nearby — playgrounds, public schools, a grocery run — or what’s true about this house. I only say what we know.

Like a home? Save it. Building a list? I’ll remember. Want to walk through? Request a showing.

I’m here on the page the whole time. Nick is one tap away — call him, or leave a note.


NOTES FOR THE READER
- “Public schools” stays. Do not say “good schools” or “safe area.”
- Do not add prices, neighborhoods as lifestyle codes, or “family-friendly.”
- If you only have one take, use PRIMARY and breathe on the dashes.
- Runway Act-Two caps a performance at 30 seconds. For this read, record the full take on your phone, then cut or use a tool that allows 45 seconds.
